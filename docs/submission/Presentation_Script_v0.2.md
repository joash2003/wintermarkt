# Linz Wintermarkt: progress presentation script (17 Nov 2026)

About 5 minutes. One block per slide of `Linz_Wintermarkt_Progress_2026-11-17.pptx`. Say it in your own words.

## 1. Title (15 s)

We are Joash, Alibek and Bobby. This is Linz Wintermarkt: you build and run a Christmas market in the Volksgarten at Goethekreuzung.

## 2. From mockup to today (25 s)

Left is our mockup from 20 October, right is the game today. The outline, paths, streets and landmarks are traced from the real map. We added snow, warm lights and a smooth park outline.

## 3. What we did since 20 October (30 s)

Four things. The real Volksgarten layout with tram and bus. A winter evening look. New rules: every visitor wants two to three things, and the score is happy visitors. And bots that test the rules.

## 4. Live demo (2 min)

1. Open the market. "The entrance is at Goethekreuzung."
2. Zoom out. "Musiktheater, Arbeiterkammer, the tram."
3. Place a Punsch Hütte and a Sweet Bakery. "I start with 400 coins."
4. Wait. "Each visitor wants two or three things and pays at every stop."
5. Point at the top bar. "The score is happy visitors. Here it says what people are missing."
6. Build the missing category.
7. Upgrade a stall with a queue. "More people served at once."
8. Escape, back to the slides.

## 5. Game mechanics and what they lead to (35 s)

The mechanics: you place and upgrade stalls and rides with coins. Visitors want two to three things, queue and pay. Reputation rises with service and falls with waiting.

That leads to this: more attraction brings more visitors and longer queues. With long queues people give up and fewer come. So the player keeps choosing between a new stall, an upgrade, or an attraction.

The feeling we want: a busy, cosy market, with pressure but in control, and proud of what you built.

And it is a loop: attraction brings visitors, service makes them happy, happy visitors raise reputation, and reputation brings more visitors.

## 6. Does it work? (25 s)

Scripted players, one full festival each. Only cheap stalls: 0 happy visitors. Attractions only: 77. One of each, then upgrades: 219. Build what visitors miss and upgrade when busy: 361. Before, attractions always won. These are scripts, not people.

## 7. What is not good yet (20 s)

Coins pile up late. You cannot see wishes on the visitors. The park is too big for the market. The map is approximate.

## 8. Future plan (30 s)

A smaller, denser market at the Goethekreuzung corner. Three waves: afternoon, rush, closing. Decorations that cost coins and raise happiness. A cosy, detailed look, with Stardew Valley as the reference. Music and sound. Playtests on 1 December and 12 January, final on 26 January.

## 9. Thank you (5 s)

Thank you. Questions?

## Likely questions

- **Why change the score?** Before, a visitor counted on arrival, even if turned away.
- **Are wishes random?** No, a fixed pattern, so runs are repeatable.
- **Is it the real Volksgarten?** Traced from Google Maps, not surveyed.
- **Do the trams do anything?** No, scenery.
- **Has anyone outside the team played it?** No. First playtest is 1 December.
