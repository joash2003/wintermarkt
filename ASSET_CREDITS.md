# Asset credits

Visuals: geometry built at runtime in MarketWorld from Unity primitives and from the models below. No purchased art or external audio is included.

| Holiday Kit 2.0 by Kenney, 23 of its 99 FBX models and its `colormap.png` texture, unmodified | `Assets/_Wintermarkt/Resources/Wintermarkt/Kit/` | https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit, downloaded 18 Nov 2026 | Creative Commons Zero (CC0); the kit's licence text is kept as `Kit/Kenney-License.txt` | Stall cabins, fir trees and the decorated Christmas tree, lanterns, snow piles, snowmen, benches, reindeer, presents and other props |

The city buildings, streets, tram, bus, rides, visitors, Krampus, lights and all interface elements are still original primitive geometry.

Audio: an original short bell tone generated at runtime in MarketGame. Font: Unity built-in LegacyRuntime font, supplied by Unity under its applicable terms. Engine, standard UI and test packages are Unity dependencies, not original Wintermarkt assets. The legacy Echo Maze source in Assets/_EchoMaze generates its own shapes, pulse rings and sine-wave beeps.

Initial code and documentation were prepared with OpenAI Codex assistance from the team's supplied handoff. Team member names and contribution attribution must be confirmed. No project-wide license has been selected; do not infer a license for redistribution from this credits file.
